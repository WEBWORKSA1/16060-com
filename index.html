Questions

What does 16060 mean?

Read aloud as “1 · 60 · 60”, it maps to time — one hour is sixty minutes of sixty seconds — and to the Chinese 60-year cycle (甲子). The digits carry positive associations: 6 (liù) evokes “smooth” (六六大顺), 0 wholeness, and 1-6 echoes 一路 (“all the way”). There is no 4. See our full explainer.

Are the tools free?

Yes. Every calculator is free, with no sign-up. The optional Lucky Blueprint asks for your email so we can send your report and follow-ups; unsubscribe any time.

How accurate is the zodiac calculator?

It uses astronomically computed Lunar New Year dates and solar terms for 1921–2060, so people born in January or February get the right animal. BaZi pillars change at the solar terms and are computed to the day.

Is this fortune-telling?

We present Chinese number symbolism and calendar traditions for cultural education and entertainment. Nothing here is financial, legal or medical advice.
